# Code of Conduct

Issues on this repository are for bug reports about the game.

* Stay on topic: one bug per issue, with the details the form asks for.
* Be civil. No insults, harassment, slurs, spam or advertising.
* Feature requests, pull requests and general discussion are not taken here (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

Anything that does not follow this may be closed, edited or deleted, and its author blocked, without discussion. To report abuse, use GitHub's *Report content*.
